## Rules

- **One kit, one capability.** If a kit needs more than seven decision points, it's two kits.
- **Nothing ships without the sanitize gate and a cold test.** A verdict under 8/10 means fix and re-run. If a kit shipped on a degraded cold test (self-review only), say so in its row — future readers need to know what was and wasn't proven.
- **Kits are living.** A kit is a repo (or a folder) with its own history; fixes are committed there, not re-exported from scratch.
- **Register the kit before you close the session.** An unregistered kit is a kit that gets rebuilt by someone else in three months.
